## 💡 Key Consumer Insights

1. **PC Hardware (Motherboards, NVMe SSDs, GPUs)**: Consistently exhibits **+15% to +28%** markups domestically. Buying from Amazon.de with free shipping to Finland saves up to 70–110 € per component.
2. **Power Tools & Home Appliances**: Domestic DIY stores (K-Rauta, etc.) charge heavy margins on professional tools (Bosch Pro, Roborock) — European mainland pricing saves 50–250 €.
3. **Smartphones (iPhone, Galaxy)**: Lowest relative Suomi-lisä (**+5% to +10%**) due to tight carrier subsidies (DNA, Elisa, Telia), though direct purchases from mainland EU still yield 70–200 € savings on Ultra/Pro models.
